# Responsible-use and data record

- All records are synthetic and contain generated identifiers only.
- The system has no network connector to issuers, acquirers, merchants or payment rails.
- Attack cards are abstract scenario parameters and omit actionable compromise steps.
- The red-team agent receives simulator outcomes only; it is never trained against a live
  third party.
- Invalid or unrealistic campaigns are denied reward by a hard fidelity firewall.
- Random seeds, configuration, source provenance and result artifacts are recorded.
- Human review is required before any scenario is promoted to a real institutional test lab.
- Operational actions include step-up and hold, avoiding a binary decline-only design.
